Markowitz mean-variance optimisation, in plain words: for every possible mix of your holdings there is an expected return and a risk (how much the return wobbles). The mixes that give the most return for each level of risk form the efficient frontier. The best mix on it is the one with the highest Sharpe ratio — return earned per unit of risk: Sharpe = (Rp − Rf) / σp, where Rp is the portfolio return, Rf the risk-free rate and σp the portfolio's volatility.
