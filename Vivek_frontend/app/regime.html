How the regime is detected

The regime is computed by deterministic rules over four inputs — trend, volatility, breadth and flows — not by gut feel:

  • Trend
    Nifty 50 vs its 50- and 200-day averages
  • Volatility
    India VIX level and direction
  • Breadth
    Share of NSE stocks above their 50-day average
  • Flows
    FII and DII net buying over 30 days

The AI only writes the explanation — the classification itself comes from the rules, so it is the same every time for the same data.

Decision support, not financial advice. Regimes describe the recent past; they do not forecast the next move.
