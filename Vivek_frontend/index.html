How it works

Three steps to a clear month

FinVerse connects the money you live on with the money you invest — so every decision starts from your real position, not a guess.

01

Tell FinVerse your month

Set your income, budgets and bills once. Add expenses in plain words — “₹500 lunch with friends” — and FinVerse files it under the right category.

02

See everything in one place

Cash flow, budgets, bills, goals, holdings and watchlist live on one dashboard. Your spending and your portfolio finally talk to each other.

03

Ask, and get an explained answer

Ask FinVerse AI anything — about your budget, a stock, or whether you are ready to invest. Every answer cites the numbers it came from.

About the project

The LLM reasons.
The code calculates.

Most finance apps hand you a score from a black-box model and ask you to trust it. FinVerse AI does the opposite: every number is computed by ordinary, auditable code, and an open-source reasoning model — from the Qwen and DeepSeek families — only interprets and explains that data, citing its evidence. A guardrail layer cross-checks every claim before you see it.

  • ✓Deterministic core. Budgets, indicators, XIRR, CAGR and Markowitz optimisation are computed, never guessed.
  • ✓Evidence-grounded AI. Function calling and retrieval (RAG) keep every explanation tied to your real data and real news.
  • ✓Cross-module intelligence. Readiness connects your month’s cash flow with your portfolio’s risk.
  • ✓Decision support, not advice. FinVerse explains your options; the decisions stay yours.

The maths, in the open

Two formulas do the heavy lifting in Module 2. Both are computed deterministically — the AI only explains what they mean for you.

Sharpe Ratio = (Rp − Rf) / σp Excess return earned per unit of risk. Higher is better.
CAGR = (Vend / Vbegin)1/n − 1 Compound annual growth rate of your portfolio over n years.
